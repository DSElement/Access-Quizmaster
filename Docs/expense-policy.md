# Expense Policy

Employees may claim up to £50 per day for meals and £150 per night for hotels when travelling on company business. All expenses must be submitted within 30 days via the Finance portal with receipts attached.

## Before booking a business trip

Obtain your manager's approval for the trip before booking transport or accommodation. Record the destination, dates, business purpose, and estimated cost in the approval request. Choose an option that meets the business need at a reasonable price. Personal holidays added to a trip must be paid for separately.

If a suitable hotel cannot be found within the £150 nightly limit, ask Finance and your manager for an exception before booking. Attach examples of available hotels and the reason the trip cannot be moved. An exception applies only to the specific booking that was approved, not to future trips.

## Meals and hotel limits

The £50 meal allowance is a daily reimbursement ceiling, not a fixed payment. Claim the amount actually spent and supported by receipts. For example, £18 for lunch and £24 for dinner produces a £42 claim. A £60 total does not automatically become reimbursable just because the trip was approved.

Meals already included in a conference ticket or hotel booking cannot be claimed again. Hotel claims cover the approved accommodation charge up to £150 per night. Personal minibar purchases, entertainment, and charges for accompanying friends or family are not reimbursable business expenses.

## Transport and other travel costs

Standard-class public transport is the default for business travel. Keep the booking confirmation or ticket showing the route, date, and price. A taxi may be claimed when public transport is unavailable, a late journey presents a practical difficulty, or equipment cannot reasonably be carried on public transport. Add the reason to the claim.

For international travel, enter the original currency and attach evidence of the amount charged in pounds if available. Finance checks the conversion used for reimbursement. Do not add an estimated exchange-rate markup or claim both the original purchase and the card payment as separate expenses.

## Submission and approval

Submit each expense within 30 calendar days of the purchase using the Finance portal. Attach an itemised receipt, the trip approval reference, and a short business explanation. Group expenses under the correct trip so the reviewer can identify the related travel dates. A bank statement alone may not show what was purchased.

Your manager reviews the business purpose; Finance checks receipts, limits, and payment details. If a claim is returned, correct the requested information in the same claim instead of submitting a duplicate. Approved claims are included in the next available reimbursement run shown in the portal.

## Missing receipts and separate budgets

If a receipt is missing, ask the supplier for a replacement. If that is impossible, explain the purchase and provide supporting evidence; Finance decides whether an exception is acceptable. Courses, books, and certification fees should use the learning-budget process in the Benefits Handbook. They are not part of the £50 daily meal allowance.
