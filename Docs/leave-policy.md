# Leave Policy

Acme Software Ltd employees receive 25 days of annual leave per year, plus 8 bank holidays. Leave requests must be submitted at least 2 weeks in advance via the HR portal.

## Entitlement and balances

The 25-day annual leave allowance applies to full-time employees working a complete leave year. Employees joining partway through the year receive a prorated allowance, recorded in the HR portal. Part-time allowances are adjusted to the employee's working pattern. The portal shows available, approved, and pending leave separately.

The 8 bank holidays are recorded separately from annual leave. Check the company calendar for the dates that apply to your assigned work location. A pending request does not mean time off has been approved. Contact HR if the displayed allowance differs from your contract or working pattern.

## Requesting planned leave

Submit planned annual leave through the HR portal at least 2 weeks before the first day off. Specify the start and end dates, any partial days, and a brief handover plan. Your line manager checks team coverage and responds through the portal. Wait for approval before making non-refundable travel arrangements.

When several employees request the same period, the manager considers customer commitments, required skills, and existing approvals. If your preferred dates cannot be approved, discuss alternatives with the manager. The helpdesk and Finance team cannot approve annual leave on a manager's behalf.

## Handover and availability

Before leave begins, identify a colleague who can cover urgent work and share the status of active tasks. Add links to relevant project notes, upcoming deadlines, and any scheduled customer meetings. Set an out-of-office message with your return date and the appropriate team contact.

Employees on approved leave are not expected to monitor routine messages. Managers should arrange coverage instead of assuming an employee will remain available. Do not include confidential customer details in an external out-of-office reply.

## Changing or cancelling a request

If your plans change, update the original request in the HR portal and notify your manager. Approved leave is not cancelled until the change is confirmed. HR adjusts the balance once the cancellation or amendment has been processed. For example, cancelling an approved three-day request returns three days to the available balance after confirmation.

If you need planned leave with less than 2 weeks' notice, explain the reason to your manager before submitting it. Short-notice approval depends on team coverage and the circumstances; it is not guaranteed. The normal notice requirement does not apply to an unexpected illness.

## Illness and other unexpected absence

Report illness to your manager as soon as practical on the first affected working day and record the absence through the HR portal. Use the sickness category rather than annual leave. HR will explain any evidence needed for an extended absence. For bereavement or another urgent personal situation, contact your manager and HR to discuss the appropriate absence category.

## Year-end questions

Review your remaining balance well before the end of the leave year and plan time off with your manager. Carryover requires HR approval; unused leave should not be assumed to transfer automatically. Benefits eligibility and probation questions are covered separately in the Benefits Handbook.
