# IT Helpdesk FAQ

To reset your VPN, disconnect the client, open a ticket at helpdesk.acme.internal, and IT will issue a new certificate within 4 hours. For urgent issues call the IT hotline on ext. 5000.

## What should I include in a support ticket?

Include your device name, operating system, affected application, and when the problem started. Describe the steps that trigger the issue and copy the exact error message. A screenshot is helpful if it does not reveal passwords, customer records, or confidential information. State whether the problem prevents all work or affects only one task.

Keep updates in the original ticket. Opening several tickets for the same issue makes it harder to track ownership and resolution. If a problem becomes urgent, call extension 5000 and give the existing ticket number so the technician can find the history.

## My VPN certificate has expired. What do I do?

Disconnect the VPN client and create a ticket at helpdesk.acme.internal requesting a certificate reset. IT will issue a new certificate within 4 hours. Follow the installation steps supplied in the ticket, then reconnect and confirm that an internal application opens. Do not reuse a certificate sent to another employee.

If the new certificate does not work, reply to the same ticket with the error text and the time of the failed connection. A VPN certificate problem is different from a CRM API key problem; resetting the VPN does not replace an application's credentials.

## How do I reset a password or recover MFA access?

Use the self-service password reset link on the company sign-in page when you still have access to your registered recovery method. If you have lost your MFA device or cannot complete verification, contact the helpdesk. IT verifies your identity before changing recovery settings. Never send a password, recovery code, or MFA approval to a technician in a ticket.

After a password reset, sign in again to applications that request updated credentials. If repeated sign-in attempts cause an account lock, stop retrying and include the lock message in your support request. Your manager can confirm your identity but cannot bypass the verification procedure.

## How do I request software or a replacement device?

Request software through the approved software catalogue. If an application is not listed, provide its name, publisher, business purpose, and manager approval in a helpdesk ticket. IT checks licensing and compatibility before installation. Do not download an unofficial installer to avoid this review.

For a damaged laptop, report the asset number and describe the fault. Back up work through the approved company storage service when the device is still usable. IT arranges diagnosis and, when needed, a temporary replacement. New-joiner equipment requests follow the Onboarding Guide.

## What counts as an urgent incident?

A lost company device, suspected phishing compromise, or outage blocking critical work should be reported immediately on extension 5000. For suspected phishing, do not click further links or delete evidence. Give the technician the message subject, sender, and whether you entered credentials. Ordinary access requests and planned software installations should use the ticket portal.
