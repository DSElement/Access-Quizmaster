# Onboarding Guide

New joiners at Acme Software Ltd should complete their IT setup, mandatory compliance training, and team introductions during their first week. Your onboarding buddy will be assigned on day one and is your first point of contact for any questions.

## Before your start date

HR sends joining instructions with your start time, work location, and manager's contact details. Confirm that your personal details are correct and complete any requested employment forms using the secure HR portal. If you have not received joining instructions two working days before your start date, contact HR rather than creating a second account.

Your manager requests the equipment and access needed for your role. Remote employees should confirm the delivery address and check that the equipment has arrived. Report missing or damaged equipment before your first day when possible so IT can arrange an alternative.

## Day one: people and accounts

Meet your manager and assigned onboarding buddy. The buddy helps you navigate the team, find internal resources, and identify the correct contact for routine questions. The manager owns your work priorities and access approvals. HR owns employment records, while IT owns device and account support.

Sign in to your company account, register your MFA method, and check access to email, the team workspace, and the HR portal. Follow the device setup instructions supplied by IT. Do not share temporary passwords or ask a colleague to lend you their account if your own access is incomplete.

## First-week IT checklist

Confirm that the laptop asset number is recorded, company updates are installed, and approved storage is available for work files. Test the VPN if your role requires remote access to internal services. For a failed VPN certificate, follow the reset procedure in the IT Helpdesk FAQ rather than repeatedly reinstalling the client.

Ask your manager which role-specific applications you need. Submit missing application access through the helpdesk with the manager's approval. A company account does not automatically grant access to every customer system or production environment.

## Training and team introductions

Complete mandatory compliance training during the first week. This includes information security, acceptable use of company systems, and handling company information. Record completion in the training portal. If a training link is unavailable, report it to HR and agree a replacement date; do not mark an unfinished course as complete.

Attend introductions with your immediate team and the colleagues you will work with most often. Ask where project documentation, meeting notes, and task boards are maintained. Your buddy can explain everyday conventions, while your manager confirms responsibilities and delivery expectations.

## First-week review and probation

At the end of the first week, review the checklist with your manager. List outstanding access requests, training tasks, and equipment problems with their ticket references. Agree initial goals and a regular check-in schedule. The standard probation period is 3 months, with feedback discussed throughout that period.

Full-time employee benefits activate after successful completion of probation, as explained in the Benefits Handbook. Annual leave requests follow the Leave Policy, and business-travel claims follow the Expense Policy. Keep those processes separate from the onboarding checklist.
