# Benefits Handbook

Acme Software Ltd offers private health insurance, a 6% pension match, and a £500 annual learning budget to all full-time employees. Benefits are activated after the 3-month probation period.

## Eligibility and activation

HR confirms successful completion of probation and sends an enrolment email with the activation date. Employees should check their personal details in the HR portal before selecting benefits. Contractors are not covered by this handbook; their benefits depend on their own contract. Part-time employees should ask HR which benefits apply to their working pattern.

If an enrolment email has not arrived within five working days of the activation date, open a benefits request in the HR portal. Include your employee number and the benefit concerned. Your onboarding buddy can help locate the form but cannot approve eligibility or change the activation date.

## Private health insurance

Eligible employees receive private health insurance for themselves. The insurer's welcome pack explains covered treatments, exclusions, and how to request authorisation. Check that pack before booking treatment; a company benefit does not mean every medical service is covered. The insurer handles medical claims, while HR handles enrolment and missing membership details.

Dependent cover is optional and may involve an employee contribution. Ask HR for the current dependent-cover options before adding a partner or child. Do not attach medical records to a general HR ticket. Use the insurer's secure claims process for clinical information.

## Pension contributions

Acme matches employee pension contributions up to 6% of pensionable salary after probation. For example, an eligible employee contributing 4% receives a 4% company match; an employee contributing 8% receives a 6% company match. Payroll confirms the pensionable salary calculation and the effective date of contribution changes.

Employees can request contribution changes through the HR portal. Submit changes before the monthly payroll cutoff shown in the portal. Check your payslip after a change and report discrepancies to Payroll, including the pay period and the expected contribution percentage.

## Annual learning budget

The £500 learning budget covers approved courses, professional books, and certification exam fees relevant to an employee's role. Request manager approval before purchasing anything. Include the provider, price, learning objectives, and how the activity supports your work. Approval of a course does not increase the annual budget.

Keep the receipt and approval reference for reimbursement. Learning purchases use the learning-budget form rather than the business-travel expense form. Unused budget does not carry into the next calendar year. Travel or hotel costs for attending a course require separate approval under the Expense Policy.

## Questions and changes

Use the HR portal for benefit eligibility, learning-budget balances, and changes in personal circumstances. Use Payroll for pension deductions. Benefits are separate from annual leave entitlement, which is explained in the Leave Policy.
