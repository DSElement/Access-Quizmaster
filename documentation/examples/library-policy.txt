Harbor Learning Library — fictional policy for quiz-generation evaluation

The Harbor Learning Library opens at 08:30 and closes at 18:00 from Monday to Friday. On Saturdays it opens at 10:00 and closes at 14:00. It is closed on Sundays. Staff announce any exceptional closure on the library noticeboard.

Membership is free for residents of Harbor. To register, an adult must bring a photo identity document and proof of their current address. Children under sixteen register with a parent or guardian. Each member receives one library card. A lost card can be replaced at the reception desk after the member confirms their identity.

Members may borrow up to three books at one time. The standard borrowing period is fourteen days. A book can be renewed once for another fourteen days if no other member has reserved it. Reference books marked with a red spine label must stay inside the library and cannot be borrowed. Members can return borrowed books through the outdoor return slot even when the building is closed.

The library does not charge overdue fines. However, a member cannot borrow additional books while any of their existing loans are overdue. A member who damages a book should tell the reception staff rather than trying to repair it. Reservations for borrowed books are free, and a reserved book is held for three opening days after staff notify the member that it is ready.

The quiet study room is on the first floor. Conversations and phone calls are not permitted there. Group work takes place in the ground-floor collaboration area. Members can reserve a collaboration table for up to two hours per day. Food is not allowed in either study area, but water in a closed bottle is permitted.

Six public computers are available beside reception. Members book a computer at the reception desk and may use it for a maximum of sixty minutes per session. Printing costs ten cents per black-and-white page. Scanning documents to a member's own USB drive is free. Staff delete files left on public computers at the end of each day.

The library runs a reading circle every Wednesday at 17:00 in the collaboration area. Attendance is free and does not require advance booking. Suggestions for new books can be placed in the wooden box beside the entrance. The library team reviews those suggestions at the end of every month.
